THE PROBLEM / THE POSSIBILITY

Too many moving parts.
Not enough visibility.

Service businesses often rely on a patchwork of messages, spreadsheets, calendars, and manual follow-ups. VEYRA is being designed to bring those workflows together.

The fragmented way

Work scattered across tools.

  • ×Customer messages spread across separate conversations.
  • ×Staff manually chase details, availability, and follow-ups.
  • ×Technician assignments depend on calls and coordination.
  • ×Campaign activity is hard to connect to completed work.
The VEYRA vision

One connected operational picture.

  • ✓Organized conversations and clearer customer context.
  • ✓Connected qualification, service, and booking workflows.
  • ✓Structured technician coordination and escalation.
  • ✓A path from inquiry source to booking and revenue.

FROM BOOKING TO FIELD WORK

A clearer path from
request to technician.

Scheduling is more than a calendar entry. The intended workflow connects customer availability, service requirements, technician eligibility, and manager escalation.

01

Confirm the service request

Understand the required service and identify the relevant location, category, and customer details.

02

Check and secure a slot

Present appropriate availability and progress the selected appointment through a controlled booking workflow.

03

Coordinate eligible technicians

Use relevant skills, location, and availability to identify who can take the job.

04

Escalate when needed

If nobody accepts within the configured window, the intended flow alerts a manager rather than silently stalling.

THE WIDER VISION

One operating model.

01 / CUSTOMER

Conversation management

02 / SALES

Lead qualification
